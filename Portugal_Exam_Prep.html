✈️ Arrivals & Transfers — The Real Trap Questions

⚠️ M&G location depends on HOW the client arrives, not just that it's their first arrival. Airport arrival → airport M&G. Arrives by ground from Spain (e.g. a private transfer Seville → Albufeira) → Hotel M&G instead. The Welcome Package is delivered to the arrival hotel either way.
⚠️ The only direct Porto ↔ Faro flight is Ryanair — a low-cost carrier that must be booked by the client directly. It cannot be booked by KT Air or the DMC. This is the one Portugal domestic route where neither usual booking channel helps.
⚠️ Porto ↔ Faro by train takes 5+ hours — not time-effective. Ties to the Ryanair rule above: this route is genuinely awkward by every mode.
⚠️ Domestic flights within Portugal are not booked by the DMCs — for mainland-to-island routes (e.g. Lisbon → Azores), KT Air assists instead.
⚠️ Azores/Madeira flights are usually TAP or Azores Airlines — Ryanair is not recommended and not bookable by KT Air.
💡 Porto ↔ Algarve: flying is recommended — much faster/more comfortable than driving or train.
⚠️ Lisbon and Porto each have multiple train stations — always specify the exact station; the DMC determines which one based on transfer arrangements. Wrong station = missed private transfer.
⚠️ Portugal has occasional rail strikes — confirm plans closer to travel and always have a backup transfer plan.
⚠️ Tourism Driver ≠ Driver-Guide — a real distinction, not synonyms, and finer than this guide's general Driver/Guide Combinations table (which lists them as one combined entry). A Tourism Driver handles transport ONLY — cannot accompany clients into sites, not even basic guiding. A Driver-Guide is one person who genuinely DOES provide basic guiding alongside driving. Always confirm whether a separate guide is needed for monument visits.
⚠️ 13-seater vans are not possible in: Douro Valley, parts of Évora, Madeira, and the Azores — narrow/winding roads. Always check routing before confirming vehicle size.
⚠️ Vehicle restrictions in historic neighborhoods: Lisbon's Alfama and Porto's Ribeira may require a short walk. Porto specifically: hotels on Rua das Flores or in Ribeira face a municipal congestion charge (€7.50–15) for vehicle drop-offs/tours — always flag this.

🏨 Hotels & Room Configuration

💡 Selecting Hotels in Portugal: hilly terrain/cobblestones can affect walkability. Historic properties trade convenience for atmosphere — always check A/C, elevators, and accessible layouts rather than assuming.
💡 Pousadas vs. Pestana: Pousadas = genuine historic character/sense of place. Pestana = modern consistency, wider range of contemporary/resort-style options. Position accordingly.
💡 Sequencing rule: start a price-conscious multi-stop itinerary at Discovery-level properties and build up to Premier — never the reverse.

📍 City-Specific Exam-Likely Facts

Lisbon

⚠️ Great Lisbon Earthquake — November 1, 1755. Massive earthquake + tsunami + fires virtually destroyed the city. Marquis of Pombal led reconstruction with earthquake-resistant buildings, wider streets, new public squares — directly shaping the flat, grid-planned Baixa seen today.
⚠️ Lisbon is home to the oldest bookshop in the world still operating — Bertrand Bookstore in Chiado, continuously open since 1732.
⚠️ Tram 28 gets crowded and pickpocket risk is real onboard — worth mentioning to clients, especially anyone carrying valuables.
💡 Orientation rule of thumb: staying close to the river gets the most out of a visit; Avenidas Novas is the strongest shopping corridor, but everything else worth prioritizing sits along the water.
Tipping: not obligatory (some restaurants add "serviço"); customary 5–10% for exceptional service; taxis/hotel staff — rounding up is common, not obligatory.
💡 "Tasca" restaurants — small family-run eateries, authentic Portuguese cooking, very reasonable prices; bustling with locals = quality signal.
⚠️ Notify credit card issuer of travel dates before departure — same as Spain.
💡 Spring (Mar–May) & Fall (Sep–Oct) = ideal window, mild temps, smaller crowds — same recommendation pattern as the Spain cities.

The Azores

💡 Routing logic: Portugal Island Gems starts in São Miguel (largest airport, direct US/Canada/mainland flights) and ends in Terceira (2nd-largest, direct + connecting flights home).

Porto

💡 For the fullest taste of local life, summer is recommended despite crowds — open-air markets fill up, and the city hosts Serralves em Festa and Festa de São João (name both).

Douro Valley

⚠️ A guided Douro Valley day tour (transportation + winery visits/tastings) runs a full ~9-hour experience. Hilly terrain/winding roads may not suit motion-sickness-prone clients.

Top Tours — Kensington Tour Levels

⚠️ Fado Night (dinner) — not available Sundays. Use the no-dinner option if that's the client's only free evening.
⚠️ Sagres Fortress / Óbidos / Fátima circuit — uneven cobblestone/limited accessibility; museums mostly closed Mondays.

🧠 Scenario Drill — Cover the Answer, Test Yourself

Q: A client wants a multi-city Portugal trip needing both a driver AND a guide. Can you use Explore Portugal for touring and Lusanova for transfers to get the best of both?
A: No. Pick ONE DMC for the entire trip — they cannot be mixed, no matter how complex the trip is.
Q: It's a Friday, client needs weekend hotel availability confirmed fast, price-conscious. DMC or Dynamic rates?
A: Dynamic rates — search and book instantly rather than waiting on the DMC.
Q: Client is flying into Lisbon for the first time. What do you arrange, and where?
A: An airport M&G at Lisbon (Humberto Delgado/LIS), plus deliver the Welcome Package to the arrival hotel.
Q: Client is being privately transferred overland from Seville to Albufeira — first arrival in Portugal. Airport or hotel M&G?
A: Hotel M&G — location depends on how they physically arrive, not just that it's a first arrival.
Q: Client wants to fly directly from Porto to Faro. Can KT Air or the DMC book it?
A: No — the only direct route is Ryanair, client must book it themselves.
Q: A driver is arranged for a Sintra day trip, no separate guide booked. Can the client get a tour of Pena Palace's interior from the driver?
A: Only if it's a Driver-Guide, not a plain Tourism Driver — a Tourism Driver cannot accompany clients into sites at all.
Q: Client staying at a hotel on Rua das Flores in Porto wants door-to-door van drop-off for every tour. Any catch?
A: Yes — a municipal congestion charge (€7.50–15) applies for vehicle drop-offs/tours in that area; flag it upfront.
Q: Hiking client wants volcanic landscapes and hot springs. Which Portugal itinerary/destination?
A: São Miguel specifically (Azores) — don't just say "the Azores."
Q: Repeat client, already done Lisbon and Porto, wants a deeper 1-week cultural trip. Portugal Revealed doesn't seem right since it still includes Lisbon — is that a mismatch?
A: No — it's still the right answer. The pitch is depth for a repeat visitor, not "avoid cities they've seen."
